What counts as an entity?

An entity is one diagnostic category in the atlas taxonomy — a WHO 2020 diagnosis, except that rhabdomyosarcoma and liposarcoma are split to molecular subtype (FP-RMS, FN-RMS, MYOD1-mutant; WD/DD/myxoid/pleomorphic/NOS) because that is the resolution the paper argues at. It is the unit each sample is assigned to.

The atlas also carries bins that are not entities, and they are excluded from every count and denominator on this page:

  • Unresolved residual bins — "Sarcoma NOS", "RMS-NOS" and two one-off cases. These hold samples whose diagnosis the deposit never states. They are a measure of metadata quality, not a disease.
  • Control tissue — non-malignant fetal osteoblast, profiled as a reference.

So: . Every "of N" figure on this page uses the named entities only. A sample in a residual bin still appears in the sample and record totals — it exists, we just cannot say what it is.
